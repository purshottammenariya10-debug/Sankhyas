About LT Foods Limited

LT Foods Limited, a fast-moving consumer goods company, engages in the specialty rice and rice-based food business in India, the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and internationally. It provides basmati and other specialty rice; organic food and ingredients; and ready to eat and ready-to-cook products. The company sells its products under the DAAWAT, Royal, Golden Star, Heritage, 817 Elephant, Rozana, Hadeel, Devaaya, Ecolife, indus valley, Leev, Kari Kari, and other brands. LT Foods Limited was incorporated in 1990 and is based in Gurugram, India.
