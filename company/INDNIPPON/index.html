About India Nippon Electricals Limited

India Nippon Electricals Limited manufactures and sells electronic ignition systems for the automotive industry in India and internationally. The company offers AC Generator/ Flywheel Magneto, Regulator and Rectifier, Integral Unit, and Ignition Coil; Controllers, such as Integrated Starter Generator and controller, electronic fuel injection, and electronic control units. It also provides position, angle, pressure, temperature, oil-level, and speed sensors. In addition, the company offers DC-DC converters, tyre pressure monitoring system, traction motors, and motor controllers. Its products have applications in two-wheelers, three-wheelers, small gasoline engines, portable gensets, general purpose engines, and non-automotive industries. The company was incorporated in 1984 and is based in Chennai, India. India Nippon Electricals Limited is a subsidiary of Lucas Indian Service Limited.
