About Best Agrolife Limited

Best Agrolife Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of agrochemical products in India and internationally. It offers insecticides, herbicides, fungicides, public-health, and plant growth regulator (PGR) products in the form of technical, intermediates, and novel formulations. The company also offers Ronfen, and insecticide; Nemagen, a novel insecticide combination; Defender, a dual-action insecticide + fungicide in WG form; Orisulam, a breakthrough herbicidal composition (GR formulation); Tricolor, a potent fungicidal formulation; and Warden Extra, a ternary SC formulation offering broad-spectrum control. In addition, it provides Tombo, a new generation corn herbicide; Azaro, a novel pre-emergence herbicide; Best Man, a deep-reaching translaminar power insecticide; Fetagen, a triple action shield, next generation insecticide; Citigen, first to launch in India post innovator; Shot Down, an comprehensive solution for the effective control of a wide range of weeds and Propique, registration-based herbicide. Margins & costs

  • Gross margins expanded by 37%, while EBITDA margins reached 20%, reflecting on the strength of our differentiated product portfolio, disciplined pricing, operational efficiency and our focused cost management.

Capex & expansion
