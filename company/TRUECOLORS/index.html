About True Colors Limited

True Colors Limited distributes digital textile inkjet printers in India. The company operates through three segments: Digital Textile Printing Services; Sublimation Paper Manufacturing; and Machinery and Ink Supply. It offers direct belt inkjet printers, double side inkjet printers, sublimation inkjet printers, heat transfer machine, pigment printers, direct to film, digital textile printing inks, such as sublimation, reactive, pigment, acid, DTF, UV, solvent and eco solvent, desktop, and flushing solvent inks. The company also manufactures and supplies sublimation papers; and offers job-work printing, and digital printed fabrics printing and supplying services. It caters to textile manufacturers, export houses, and fabric dealers; and installation services, spare parts, and after-sales support. It serves publishers, offset printers, educational institutions, copy shops, short-run print, pharma, food and consumer goods, industrial packaging, label converters, and packaging print industries. The company was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Surat, India.
