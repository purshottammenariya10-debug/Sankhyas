About Kanoria Energy & Infrastructure Limited

Kanoria Energy & Infrastructure Limited manufactures and sells asbestos cement pressure pipes, asbestos cement corrugated roofing sheets, and allied products in India. The company offers asbestos cement pressure pipes for drinking water, sewage and drainage, and irrigation purposes. It also provides couplings; asbestos cement corrugated sheets; asbestos cement sheets and molded goods; and asbestos cement tree guard products. In addition, the company is involved in the laying and jointing of asbestos cement products. It sells its products under the JAI KIRTI brand. The company was formerly known as A Infrastructure Limited and changed its name to Kanoria Energy & Infrastructure Limited in April 2023. Kanoria Energy & Infrastructure Limited was incorporated in 1980 and is based in New Delhi, India.
