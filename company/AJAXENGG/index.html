About Ajax Engineering Limited

Ajax Engineering Limited manufactures and sells self-loading concrete mixers, concrete batching plants, and concrete pumps in India and internationally. The company also offers transit mixers, stationary concrete pumps, boom pumps, self-propelled boom pumps, 3D concrete printers, and concrete slip-form pavers, as well as dumpers, planetary mixers, and twin shaft mixers. It serves roadways, waterways, irrigation, renewable energy, railways, residential/commercial, airports, and urban infrastructure sectors. Ajax Engineering Limited was incorporated in 1992 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
