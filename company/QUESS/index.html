About Quess Corp Limited

Quess Corp Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides staffing and workforce solutions in India and internationally. It operates through four segments: General Staffing, Professional Staffing, Overseas Staffing, and Digital Platforms. The company offers general staffing, such as sourcing and recruitment, apprenticeship program, and center of experience; and flexi staffing, payroll and compliance management, apprenticeship solutions, gig workforce management, and workforce lifecycle services. It also provides professional staffing, including digital technology skills, GCC services, recruitment services, and mobility solutions; and niche talent acquisition, project staffing, contractual hiring, and specialist recruitment across technology domains, including AI, cloud, analytics, cybersecurity, and digital engineering. In addition, the company offers international staffing and workforce mobility solutions in APAC markets in engineering, healthcare, infrastructure, construction, hospitality, and services sectors through sourcing, deployment, and workforce management solutions.
