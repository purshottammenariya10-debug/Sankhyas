About Tata Elxsi Limited

Tata Elxsi Limited provides product design and engineering, systems integration, and support services in India, the United States, Europe, and Internationally. The company operates through two segments, Systems Integration and Support, and Software Development and Services. It offers iCX, a SaaS-based solution for automating, monitoring, and managing CPEs; and AIVA, an AI-based video analytics platform that automates video tagging, object detection, content indexing, and metadata generation. It also provides QoEtient, a platform for video streaming services; TEPlay, an OTT/IPTV platform; Neuron, an autonomous network platform for telco operators; and uLike, a platform for AI-powered content recommendation engine. In addition, it offers TETHER, a cloud-based platform for vehicle connectivity; eMobility HILS, a scalable solution for validating electric vehicle systems; Robo-Taxi, a platform for autonomous ride-hailing; AVENIR, a platform for real-time data processing, validation, and integration for software defined vehicles; AutonomAI, an AI-driven platform for self-driving capabilities; and MOBIUS+, a blockchain-powered solution.
