About Fabtech Technologies Limited

Fabtech Technologies Limited operates as a biopharma engineering company that provides aseptic manufacturing facilities internationally. The company offers designing, engineering, procurement, installation, and testing of pharmaceutical equipment. It serves pharmaceuticals, biotech, and healthcare companies. The company was founded in 2001 and is based in Mumbai, India.
