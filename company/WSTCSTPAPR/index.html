About West Coast Paper Mills Limited

West Coast Paper Mills Limited manufactures and sells pulp, paper, and paper boards in India and internationally. It operates through Paper, Cables, and Others segments. The company offers printing and writing paper, machine-glazed paper and boards, security and hi-value grades paper, cup stock and coated duplex boards, business stationery products, and premium printing paper under the WESCO brand, as well as optical fibers and optical fiber cables. It is also involved in the wind power generation business with a capacity of 74.8 megawatts. The company sells its products to the B2B and B2C markets through a network of distributors; banks; the printing, writing, publishing, stationery, notebook, packaging, telecommunications, infrastructure development, data center, utilities, and defense sectors; and government institutions. It exports its products to approximately 20 countries across the Asia Pacific, the Middle East, the Mediterranean, and the African subcontinents. The company was formerly known as The West Coast Paper Mills Limited and changed its name to West Coast Paper Mills Limited in September 2016.
