About Apollo Pipes Limited

Apollo Pipes Limited manufactures and trades in polyvinyl chloride (PVC) pipes and fittings in India. The company offers cPVC, uPVC, and PPR-C plumbing systems; uPVC pressure pipes and fittings, and garden pipes; HDPE PLB duct pipes and MDPE and HDPE gas piping system; SWR drainage system, underground drainage system, and DWC pipes; and agri pipes and fitting, HDPE pipes, coils, and sprinkler systems. It also provides underground drainage uPVC column Pipes, and casing pipes; water tanks, solvent cement, and kitchen sink. In addition, the company offers bath fittings, which include faucets, showers, health faucets, cistern, seat covers, accessories, and allied products. It provides its products to plumbing, agriculture, sewerage, water management, construction, fenestration industry, infrastructure, borewell system solutions, water storage solutions, water supply solutions, bath fittings solutions, industrial piping solutions, and telecom ducting industries. Apollo Pipes Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is headquartered in Noida, India.

Concall summary (2026-08-03)

AI summary of the earnings call transcript · tone: Positive · source document

Guidance & outlook

  • The volatility in PVC resin prices was even higher in Q1 FY27 with prices falling by Rs.32 per Kg in April followed by a little in May, but prices fell again by Rs.5 per Kg in June.
  • For Window profile, we expect that business to contribute 7% to 8% to our revenue, okay, in FY27 and gradually, it will keep on improving and maybe it will settle at around 10% as per the current capacity.

Growth & demand

  • Needless to say that both primary and secondary demand in April were worst due to extreme price drop of 30% in first 20 days, but it started to recover May onwards.
  • So, in terms of volume, we are confident of high double-digit volume growth for next coming years, including FY27.

Margins & costs

  • Our normalized business EBITDA margins were 7% on consolidated basis.
  • So, at the business level, we believe that 7% to 8% EBITDA margin for the next 12 to 15-months is what we are going to achieve.

Capex & expansion

  • This will be supported by our newly commissioned Varanasi plant and continuous ramp-up of Maharashtra plant.
  • Once the current capacity gets consumed, then we will expand more capacity and the business has potential to be like 15% to the overall Apollo Pipes portfolio.

Balance sheet & cash

  • I mean, all this CAPEX of Rs.500-600 crores in the last two-three years was done from internal cash flow and fundraise from the promoters and a foreign fund.
  • So, I think 70%-80% will be met from our internal cash flows, and if there is any requirement of more funds, a balance sheet of that size can manage some debt or we will raise equity.
