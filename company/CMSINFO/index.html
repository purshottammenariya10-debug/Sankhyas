About CMS Info Systems Limited

CMS Info Systems Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides cash management and managed services in India. It operates in three segments: Cash Management Services, Managed Services, and Cards. The company offers cash management services comprising ATM cash management services, such as cash processing, cassette management, ATM replenishment, cash evacuation for banknote accepting/recycling, day-end reporting, and reconciliation; retail cash management services, including cash pickup and deposit logistics, Cash-X electronic, and retail cash vault solutions; and other related services, as well as cash-in-transit services for banks. It also provides managed services, such as banking automation services comprising ATM manufacturing and maintenance, currency recyclers, and self-service kiosks; brown label ATM deployment and managed services for banks; software solutions, including ATM multi-vendor software and automated ATM security software solutions; and remote monitoring technology solutions.
