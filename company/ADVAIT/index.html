About Advait Energy Transitions Limited

Advait Energy Transitions Limited provides products and solutions for power transmission, substation, and telecommunication infrastructure sectors in India and internationally. It operates through the Power Transimission Service, and New and Renewable Energy segments. The company manufactures and supplies stringing tools, optical ground wires (OPGW), optical fibre ground cables, aluminum clad steel wires, emergency restoration systems, revamped distribution sector scheme, OPGW joint boxes, re-conductoring and high temperature low sag, and joint boxes, as well as glass disk, porcelain, and composite insulators.
