About Tembo Global Industries Limited

Tembo Global Industries Limited engages in manufacturing, fabrication, jobbing and trading of engineering products in India and internationally. The company offers anti-vibration solutions, channel support, dielectric union, floor drain, hanging attachments, pipe hangers, roller supports, rubber supports inserts, single pipe, traverse brace, solar accessories, solar mounting brackets, and threaded rod and fasteners; and stainless-steel flanges, as well as various types of bathroom pipes, fittings, bathroom accessories, and sanitary wares. It also manufactures and exports textile products, such as fibre and yarns; suiting fabrics; bed sheets and handkerchiefs; and shirting, uniform, and knitted fabrics, as well as trades in metal, fabrics, and textile products. Its products have applications in pipe support systems, fasteners, anchors, HVAC, anti-vibration systems and equipment for industrial, commercial, utility, and OEM installations. The company was formerly known as Saketh Exim Limited. Tembo Global Industries Limited was incorporated in 2010 and is based in Navi Mumbai, India.
