About India Pesticides Limited

India Pesticides Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the agrochemicals business in India and internationally. The company offers formulations such as herbicides, fungicides, insecticides, acaricides, and growth regulators; and active pharmaceutical ingredients, including anti-fungal and anti-scabies drugs. It also provides generic agrochemical technicals comprising herbicide technicals, which consists of prosulfocarb, pretilachlor, flufenacet, and aclonifen; fungicide technicals, including cymoxanil, captan, folpet, ziram, dodine, thiophanate-methyl, and thiram; insecticide technicals, which include diafenthiuron, pyriproxyfen, and thiamethoxam; and intermediate technicals, such as PEDA, pthalamide, and tetra hydro pthalamide. In addition, the company offers custom synthesis and contract manufacturing services for various active ingredients and intermediates. It sells its products through its sales force. The company serves manufacturers, formulators, traders, exporters, and MNCs.
