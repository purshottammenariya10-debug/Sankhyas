About Spencer's Retail Limited

Spencer's Retail Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in developing, conducting, and promoting organised retail and operates departmental and neighbourhood stores under various formats across the country in India. It offers products in various categories, such as food, including fruits and vegetables, staples, beverages, processed food, dairy, fish and meat, and spices and condiments; personal care comprising skin care, hair care, oral care, baby care, men's grooming, and personal hygiene; home, including home decor home furnishings, bathroom essentials, kitchen essentials, dining essentials, travel needs, and toys and stationery; and electronics and electricals, such as home appliances, kitchen appliances, electrical fittings and accessories, and digital equipment and accessories. The company also provides fashion and accessories, including men's casual and office wear, and women's casual, ethnic, innerwear, accessories, footwear; organic food diet food, pet food, baby food, nuts and dry fruits, and party delights; and gourmet, wine and spirits, tobacconist, epicuisine, and nutty delights.
