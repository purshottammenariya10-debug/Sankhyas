About Servotech Renewable Power System Limited

Servotech Renewable Power System Limited manufactures and sells light-emitting diode (LED) lights, electric vehicle (EV) chargers, and solar power products in India and internationally. The company's products include EV chargers, solar products, such as solar inverter, servport, solar management unit, solar batteries, solar lithium battery, and solar panels; servo and backup, including battery and stabilizer and UPS; lithium battery and electric 3W charger; domestic and commercial LEDs; and oxygen concentrator and UVC. It also provides solar EPC, calculator and EV calculator. In addition, the company offers UV-C handheld disinfection lamp, portable UV-C disinfection lamp, portable UV-C disinfection lamp, UV-C disinfection robotruk, UV-C sterilization bag, UV-C LED sterilization box, UV sterilization box with charger, UV-C car intelligent sanitizer, car air sanitizer, UV air purifier, and FAR UV-C digital sanitizer. Further, it provides electric vehicle charging components, such as power modules, control circuits, and PLCs. Additionally, the company engages in sports, events, and promotion business.
