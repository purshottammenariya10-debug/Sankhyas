About Transrail Lighting Limited

Transrail Lighting Limited, an engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company, engages in the designing, engineering, supply, manufacture, construction, testing services for power transmission and distribution in India and internationally. The company also provides EPC for civil construction, such as bridges, tunnels, elevated roads, and cooling towers; solutions for solar power projects including Solar EPC, BESS, hybrid projects, pooling substations, and grid connectivity; supply, installation, testing, and commissioning service for poles and lighting; and overhead electrification, signaling and telecommunication services, earthwork, track linking, and other composite work for railway. It serves central and state governments, public sector undertakings, ministries and local municipal bodies, and private sectors. The company was founded in 1984 and is based in Mumbai, India. Transrail Lighting Limited operates as a subsidiary of Ajanma Holdings Private Limited.
