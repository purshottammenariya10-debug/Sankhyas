About Isgec Heavy Engineering Limited

Isgec Heavy Engineering Limited provides engineering solutions worldwide. It operates in two segments, Manufacturing of Machinery & Equipment; and Industrial Projects. The company provides process plant equipment, including reactors, high pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, columns and towers, and high pressure boiler drums; boilers, such as solid and biomass fuels, bagasse and biomass, oil and gas fired, blast furnace gas fired, waste to energy, and heat recovery boilers, as well as auxiliaries comprising air pollution control equipment, bag filters, SCR/SNCR, flue gas desulphurization systems, and deaerators; and site construction and commissioning, operation and maintenance, supervision, retrofitting and modernization, residual life assessment studies, spares, and technical assistance services. Guidance & outlook

  • Export revenue during the quarter is INR 385 crores, about 25% of the total revenue, up from 15% in Q1 FY26.
  • The plant is presently running at about 65% capacity, and the current quarter is expected to be better.

Growth & demand

  • Standalone Financial Performance We delivered strong growth this quarter with the total income up 51% and PBT up 10% year- on-year.
  • Total order booking for the first quarter is INR 2,323 crores and the total standalone orders in hand as on June 30, 2026, are very good at INR 7,727 crores.

Margins & costs

  • On the margin front, the manufacturing EBIT margins continued to be 12% and within the 12% to 13% range guided by us.
