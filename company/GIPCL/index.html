About Gujarat Industries Power Company Limited

Gujarat Industries Power Company Limited engages in the generation, transmission, and distribution of electricity to power purchasing companies in India. The company generates power through gas, lignite, wind, and solar power plants with combined installed capacity of 1859.40 MW, as well as engages in the development of hybrid renewable energy parks. The company was incorporated in 1985 and is based in Vadodara, India.
