About Mahindra EPC Irrigation Limited

Mahindra EPC Irrigation Limited manufactures, sells, and markets micro irrigation systems in India and Uganda. The company's drip irrigation products include online drip irrigation products, such as drippers and plain irrigation lateral products; and inline drip irrigation products comprising non-pressure compensating (PC) flat, non-PC round, PC flat, and LITE products. It also offers drip irrigation components consisting of plastic and metallic hydro-cylone, vertical and horizontal sand, plastic and metal shell screen, disc, and semi-automatic screen filters; metallic and plastic header assemblies; venturies and fertilizer tanks; PP ball, butterfly, non-return, and PP flush control valves; pressure relief, vacuum breaker, and air release safety valves; and aquafit laterals. In addition, the company provides sprinkler irrigation products, such as various sprinklers, quick pipes coupling sprinkler pipes and fittings, and rainguns; automation systems; protected cultivation; HDPE pipes and coils; and DIY kits, including water reservoir, tubing, drip emitters, and fittings.
