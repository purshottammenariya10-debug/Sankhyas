About IKIO Technologies Limited

IKIO Technologies Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells light emitting diode lighting solutions in India and internationally. It offers ODM for LED lights; switches and hardware components; LED refrigeration lights and controls; recreational vehicle components; and LED drivers. The company also provides LED lighting solutions, including architectural lighting, product display and refrigeration systems, and RV and mobility solutions; automotive LED lighting; consumer electronics, such as earbuds, speakers, smartwatches, headsets, and neckbands; OEM/ODM products; and electronics and energy systems. In addition, it engages in the design, fabrication, and assembly of mechanical components for lighting, solar, and electronic manufacturing services; and product display lighting, IPS controllers, and specialized electrical components for commercial refrigeration. The company's products are used in residential, commercial, and industrial applications. It exports its products.
