About Chalet Hotels Limited

Chalet Hotels Limited owns, develops, manages, and operates hotels and resorts in India. It also operates in rental and annuity, and real estate development business. The company's portfolio comprises hotels and commercial spaces. In addition, it engages in construction and development of residential properties. Chalet Hotels Limited was incorporated in 1986 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.

Concall summary (2026-08-05)

AI summary of the earnings call transcript · tone: Positive · source document

Guidance & outlook

  • EBITDA stood at INR735 million, up 21% year -on-year, with an EBITDA margin of 85%, up 193 bps versus Quarter 1 FY2026.
  • We expect monthly rentals to scale up to INR 300 - 320 million during FY2027.

Growth & demand

  • Leisure portfolio delivered a strong 19% RevPAR growth with a healthy mix of occupancy and ADR increase.
  • There have been also upgrades that have aided us in the revenue growth, like addition of Nox, a sky lounge that used to be a dead space and now clocks ~INR10 million a month in revenue within the first year of opening, addition of gaming and sports facilities, and more.

Margins & costs

  • Importantly, EBITDA margin improved by 231 bps to 46.7%.
  • Hospitality revenue grew by 9%, while EBITDA stood at INR1,784 million, up 11% year -on- year, reflecting 92 bps improvement in EBITDA margin on the back of resilient pricing dynamics and efficiency measures.

Capex & expansion

  • Several more changes are work in progress, including the widening of the approach road and the expansion of the banquet facility, which alone will 3x our facilities and become one of the largest in the MMR.
  • As I have mentioned, commissioning of CIGNUS II at our Powai complex in FY2027-28 will bring a major impetus to this business.

Balance sheet & cash

  • The current yearly rentals potentially give us a leverage advantage of INR2,000 crores eligibility for LRD.
  • INR10,914 million of the net debt is actually allocable to assets under construction or which are yet to be operationalized.
