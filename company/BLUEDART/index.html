About Blue Dart Express Limited

Blue Dart Express Limited provides courier and express services in India. Its domestic express portfolio includes time-definite delivery services, such as Domestic Priority 1030 and Domestic Priority 1200, and day-definite delivery solutions, including DP-Dox, DP Parcel, Air Express Package, Surface B2B, eCom Air, eCom Lite Surface, and eCom Surface. The company also offers air freight services between the airports of Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad; ecom lite solution, a speed trucking solution; air express and ground express packaging services under the Smart Box name; express palette, a wood-free palletized packaging; shipment for freight of 50kg, 75kg, or above 100kg; door-to-door express deliveries for documents and shipments; international services; and temperature-controlled logistics solutions, as well as operates domestic express airline charter. It has a fleet of 6 Boeing 757-200 freighters and 2 Boeing 737 aircraft; and a fleet of e-vehicles in India. Blue Dart Express Limited was founded in 1983 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India. Blue Dart Express Limited is a subsidiary of DHL Express (Singapore) Pte. Ltd.
