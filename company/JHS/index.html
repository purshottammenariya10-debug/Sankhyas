About JHS Svendgaard Laboratories Limited

JHS Svendgaard Laboratories Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures, trades in, and sells a range of oral and dental products in India. The company offers oral care products, such as toothbrushes, toothpaste, mouthwash products, whitening gels, talcum powders, personal care products, sanitizers, detergent powders, and denture tablets. It also provides contract manufacturing services for oral care products. In addition, the company exports its products. JHS Svendgaard Laboratories Limited was founded in 1997 and is based in New Delhi, India.
