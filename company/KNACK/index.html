About Knack Packaging Limited

Knack Packaging Limited operates as a packaging solutions provider. The company manufactures Printed and Laminated Woven Polypropylene (PLWPP) bags and PLWPP Pinch Bottom bags. The company's products include High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) /Polypropylene (PP) Tapes, HDPE/PP Woven Fabrics, HDPE/PP Woven Bags, Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene (BOPP) Laminated PP Woven Bags, BOPP Laminated PP Woven Pinch Bottom Bags, BOPP Laminated PP Block Bottom Bags, BOPP Laminated PP Woven Bottom Gusset Bags, Shopping Bags, Multi-Filament Yarn, Narrow Woven Fabrics, Design and Cylinder Services, and Reprocessed Granules. The company also provides add-on solutions such as circular & back seam construction, half, full & register window, zigzag cut, and heatcut & bladecut. It offers packaging solutions for a wide range of sectors, including food products, such as grains and pulses  rice, dal, lentils, etc., flour & spices, sugar, salts, fruits & nuts, animal & pet food, and agriculture, seeds, charcoal, detergents powders & granules, fertilizers, chemicals, cement, tile adhesives, building materials, and mineral bags.
