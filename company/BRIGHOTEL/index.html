About Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited

Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited engages in the development and operation of hotels in India. The company is involved in the operation, management, and leasing of hotels. The company was founded in 2004 and is based in Bengaluru, India. Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited operates as a subsidiary of Brigade Enterprises Limited.
