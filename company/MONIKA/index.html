About Monika Alcobev Limited

Monika Alcobev Limited engages in importing, sales, distribution, and marketing for spirits, wines, and liqueurs in India and Internationally. The company offers brandy and cognac, gin, rum, tequila, mezcal, soju, pisco, vodka, whiskey, wine, bitters and liqueur. Monika Alcobev Limited was founded in 2015 and is based in Mumbai, India..
