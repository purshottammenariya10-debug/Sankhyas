About Credo Brands Marketing Limited

Credo Brands Marketing Limited engages in the retail of men's casual wear under the MUFTI brand in India. The company offers athleisure merchandise, shirts, jeans, cargos, polos, t-shirts, sweatshirts, sweaters, outerwear, blazers, chinos, and trousers, as well as authentic, relaxed, and urban casuals. It also operates an online shop for its products. The company sells its products through multi-brand and exclusive brand outlets, large format stores, and online channels comprising its website and e-commerce marketplaces. The company was founded in 1998 and is based in Mumbai, India.
