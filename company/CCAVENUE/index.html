About AvenuesAI Limited

AvenuesAI Limited, a payment infrastructure company, provides digital payments and lending solutions for merchants, enterprises, corporations, governments, and financial institutions in India, the United Arab Emirates, and internationally. The company offers CCAvenue, a payment gateway that enables merchants to accept payments through websites and mobile devices; BillAvenue, a unified and interoperable bill-payment layer for utility and recurring payments; ResAvenue, an integrated digital hospitality platform; and Go Payments, which enables customers to access digital services through nearby physical touchpoints. It also provides B2Biz Payments, which automates business collections and payouts through unified workflows; Invoice Payments, which enables merchants to create and share itemized invoices, and collect online payments; TapPay / POS, which extends omnichannel acceptance into offline commerce; Subscriptions, which creates automated recurring-payment plans with configurable billing cycles, payment modes, and event notifications for subscription businesses; CCAvenue TokenPay, which provides network-token based card-on-file capability for compliant card storage and processing;…
