About Skipper Limited

Skipper Limited manufactures and sells transmission and distribution structures, telecom towers, and fasteners in India. The company operates through three segments: Engineering Products, Infrastructure Projects, and Polymer Products. The Engineering Products segment offers power transmission towers, telecom towers, tower accessories, fasteners, angles, channels, high mast and swaged poles, solar power systems, railway structures, as well as power distribution poles, transmission lines, monopoles, mild steel and high tensile angles, and other products. The Infrastructure Projects segment provides water engineering, procurement, and construction services for telecom, tower, and water, as well as coating services. The Polymer Products segment offers PVC, HDPE, CPVC, UPVC, SWR pipes and fittings, water tanks, bath fittings, and other related products, as well as agriculture pipes, borewell pipes and fittings, and solvent cement. The company also exports its products to approximately 65 countries covering South America, Africa, the Middle East, Australia, New Zealand, Europe, and South and South East Asia. The company was incorporated in 1981 and is based in Kolkata, India.
