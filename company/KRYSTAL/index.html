About Krystal Integrated Services Limited

Krystal Integrated Services Limited provides integrated and technical facility management, staffing, payroll and solid and liquid waste management, private security, manned guarding, solar EPC, and catering services in India. It operates through the Manpower and Related Service, Catering Service, and Information Technology Enabled Services segments. The company offers housekeeping, janitorial, disinfection and sanitation, landscaping, industrial and commercial cleaning, pest control, and hygiene services; mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) maintenance services; solid, liquid and biomedical waste management, façade cleaning, HVAC maintenance, fire safety system inspections, and other engineering services; and warehouse management, and airport management services, including airport housekeeping, multi-level parking management, and airport traffic management services. It also provides staffing and payroll management services, such as recruitment, payroll processing, temporary staffing, and executive search; production support for manufacturing, logistics, and material handling operations; surveillance, access control, security audits, event security, and traffic management…
