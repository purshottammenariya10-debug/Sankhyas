About GRP Limited

GRP Limited manufactures and sells reclaimed rubber products in India and internationally. It operates in Reclaim Rubber and Others segments. The company offers reclaim natural and synthetic rubber products for use in inner liners and tubes, tyre side walls and piles, tyre treads and retreads, conveyor belts, moulded goods, adhesives, and footwear, as well as mattings, profiles, and roofing; and POLYCOAT, an elastic powder in compounded form, which is used in NBR based molded products, metal to rubber bonded parts, NBR based oil seals, NBR matting/sheeting, and NBR-PVC blend. It also provides engineering plastics comprising glass filled and unfilled reprocessed nylon 6, nylon 66, and PBT used in automotive, electrical and electronics, consumer, and industrial applications; repurposed polyolefins used in paint and lubricant pails, automotive, furniture, battery covers, and electricals; custom die forms; heavy duty wheel chock and coil saddle for coil storage and transport; rear bumper guard use in warehousing, logistics, and docks; and car parking stopper. AI summary of the earnings call transcript · tone: Positive · source document

Guidance & outlook

  • In our tire recycling businesses for Reclaim Rubber, for example, we expect volumes in FY 2027 to grow by close to 20% over the prior year.
  • This is well ahead of our stated target of 50% by FY 2028, which we should reach in the current fiscal.

Growth & demand

  • The consolidated total income for the quarter grew 26% year -on-year to INR1,573 million on the back of volume growth, mainly attributable to the Pyrova Energy capacity utilization and improving realizations with both domestic and export markets contributing meaningfully.
  • On a stand-alone basis, the segment revenue grew 34%, of which Reclaim volumes grew 12% GRP Limited July 27, 2026 and reclaim export volumes rebounded 20%, as customer order patterns normalized following the easing of U.S. tariffs.

Margins & costs

  • Importantly, that growth led to an EBITDA rise of 60% to INR174 million and EBITDA margin expansion by 233 basis points to 11%, even as raw material costs rose sharply during the quarter.
  • Together, our plastic verticals expanded EBITDA margins by 14 percentage points year -on- year on a 10-point improvement in gross margin, reflecting a better product and customer mix and continued cost discipline.

Capex & expansion

  • During the quarter, the plant achieved its longest continuous reactor run since commissioning, an important milestone because stable, continuous operation is the precursor to throughput, reliability, and commercial performance in a continuous pyrolysis process.
  • On profitability, we expect margins to build from a single digit during the ramp -up stage that we are currently in to move towards 18% to 20% once the rCB facility is commissioned, operational and mature with customer approvals.

Balance sheet & cash

  • In the quarter of significant input cost inflation, operating leverage, product mix and cost discipline did the heavy lifting.
  • Beyond the operating performance, we improved our working capital cycle by 8 days to 86 days, reflecting continued operational discipline.
