About VMS TMT Limited

VMS TMT Limited manufactures and sells thermo-mechanically treated high-strength reinforcement steel bars in India. The company offers TMT bars under the Kamdhenu NXT brand name; and sells scrap, binding wires and billets. VMS TMT Limited was incorporated in 2013 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
