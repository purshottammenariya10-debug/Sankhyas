About Travel Food Services Limited

Travel Food Services Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manages and operates travel quick service restaurant outlets and lounges in India and internationally. The company engages in the operation of fast foods, cafes, bakeries, restaurants, and bars, as well as airport lounges. It is also involved in the development and management of technology infrastructure and related services for airport operations; provision of food, beverage, and hospitality services; consultancy services; and investment in commercial enterprises, as well as management activities. The company was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Mumbai, India. Travel Food Services Limited operates as a subsidiary of Ssp Asia Pacific Holdings Limited.
