About Protean eGov Technologies Limited

Protean eGov Technologies Limited, together with its subsidiaries, develops and implements citizen-centric and population-scale e-governance solutions and digital public infrastructure in India. It offers PAN and tax deduction, and collection account number issuance; electronic tax deducted at source return processing; online PAN verification; digital enrolment, Electronic National Pension System, and D-Remit; and digital permanent retirement account number generation and integrated payment solutions. The company also provides digital identity services, such as authentication, verification and paperless transactions; and identity solutions, including Aadhaar authentication, e-KYC services, licensed eSign services, and online PAN verification. In addition, it offers Open Network for Digital Commerce, a BECKN gateway and registry; Central AgriStack for data exchange of farmers, government bodies, and private entities; Vidhyasaarthi and ONEST for education and skilling; CMIS and PHR applications, as well as centralized dashboards for the health sector; and technology platform for a digital insurance marketplace.
