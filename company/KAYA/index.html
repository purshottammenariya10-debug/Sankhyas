About Kaya Limited

Kaya Limited engages in the provision of skin and hair care solutions through scientific dermatological procedures and products in India. The company offers skin care products, such as facewash, cleansers, toners, moisturizers, creams, face serums, sunscreen lotions, kits and regimes, wet wipes, and lip care; hair care products that include shampoos, hair serums, hair lotions, hair oils, and hair masques; bath and body products; and nutraceutical products. It also provides skin services comprising laser hair reduction, thermage, HIFU, dermal fillers, chemical peel, Q-switch laser, and skin brightening and lightening treatments, as well as ageing skin, acne scars, skin IV drips, dark circle, anti-wrinkle, and microneedling; hair services, such as hair loss concern management, PRP GF, hair thread, QR678, hair loss in men, hair loss and thinning for women, and hair IV drips solutions; and body contouring services consisting of CM slim, Venus legacy, CoolSculpting, weight loss IV drips, face fat reduction treatment, and Artiqa.
