About CIE Automotive India Limited

CIE Automotive India Limited produces and sells automotive components to original equipment manufacturers and other customers in India, Europe, and internationally. It offers forgings, such as crankshafts, CV joints, knuckles, diff Casw, and spindles; gears, including gears and shafts, e-drive components, flanges/end yokes, and clutch hubs; composites comprising front lid, wind shield-electric-3WH, electric box top tray, and front bumper; and crankcase, pump housing, and brake panel aluminum products. The company also provides iron castings, such as differential, turbine, and axle housings, as well as gear carriers; hard and soft magnets; and stampings, including chassis and structural parts, BIW panels and assemblies, cross car beam, safety assemblies, and fuel tanks. In addition, it offers stub axle as forged and machined, steering shaft, wheel hubs, steering yokes, constant velocity joints, steel metal stamping, components and assemblies; turbo chargers housing; axle and transmission parts; and engine gear, timing gear, and drive shafts.
