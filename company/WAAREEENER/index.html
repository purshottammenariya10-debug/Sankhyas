About Waaree Energies Limited

Waaree Energies Limited manufactures and trades in solar photovoltaic modules in India and internationally. It operates through Solar Photovoltaic Modules; Generation of Power; and Engineering, Procurement and Construction Contract segments. The company offers PV modules, such as HJT, N Type, mono PERC, flexible, and special modules; single and three phase on-grid inverter; solar kits; battery energy storage system; electrolyzer stacks; smart meter; transformer; and Waaree lights. It also provides engineering, procurement, and construction services; asset management; and solar systems for emerging, hybrid, and niche formats, including tracker-integrated systems, agrovoltaics, BIPV, and repowering legacy plants. The company serves engineering, procurement and construction companies; power production companies; power generation utilities; and individual consumers. The company was founded in 1989 and is based in Mumbai, India.
