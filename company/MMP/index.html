About MMP Industries Limited

MMP Industries Limited engages in the manufacture, sale, distribution, and trade of aluminium products in India. It operates in four segments: Aluminium Powders and Pastes, Aluminium Conductors, Aluminium Foils, Insulators, and Others. The company offers pyro, flake, and atomized aluminum powders for use in various sectors, including construction, mining and infrastructure, agriculture, defense, pyrotechnics and fireworks, railways, and specialized industrial applications; aluminum pastes; and aluminum conductors and cables used by the power sector. It also provides aluminum foils that are used in pharmaceutical and food packaging applications; polymer insulators, ceramic insulators, FRP rods, and others; and manganese oxide/dioxide, washers, circlips, and coal products. In addition, the company engages in trading and manufacturing manganese oxide and manganese dioxide powder. It exports its products to countries in Europe, the Middle East, other Asian markets, and Africa. The company was formerly known as Maharashtra Metal Powders Limited and changed its name to MMP Industries Limited in June 2012. MMP Industries Limited was incorporated in 1973 and is based in Nagpur, India.
