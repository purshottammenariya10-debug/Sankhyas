About Captain Polyplast Limited

Captain Polyplast Limited manufactures, sells, exports, and trades in micro irrigation systems and allied products in India. The company provides irrigation equipment, such as drip irrigation systems, drippers and laterals, and sprinklers; irrigation accessories comprising compression fittings, control valves, irrigation filters, fertigation equipment, greenhouses, and mulch films; solar systems consisting of solar water pumps, solar fence energizers, solar water heaters, and solar streetlight systems; water-soluble fertilizers; and pipes and fittings. It also offers high density polyethylene, linear low density polyethylene, polypropylene homopolymer, and polypropylene copolymer polymer products under the Propel brand for packaging, pipes, films, molding, etc. applications. In addition, the company provides mulch films, pressure gauges, and venturi injectors. Captain Polyplast Limited was incorporated in 1997 and is headquartered in Rajkot, India.
