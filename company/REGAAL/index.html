About Regaal Resources Limited

Regaal Resources Limited operates as a maize milling company that manufactures and sells maize starch and its derivatives in India. The company offers starches, such as native starches maize and oxidized starch, yellow and white dextrin, and organic gulal. It also provides corn flour, icing sugar, baking and custard powder; liquid glucose and maltodextrin powder; maize germ, maize gluten, maize fibre, maize enrich fiber, maize steep liquor; and dextrose anhydrous and monohydrate, cationic starch, carboxymethyl starch, pregel starch, and spray starch. The company was incorporated in 2012 and is headquartered in Kolkata, India.
