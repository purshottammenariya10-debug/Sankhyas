About Intellect Design Arena Limited

Intellect Design Arena Limited engages in the development, marketing, and sale of integrated banking software systems in India and internationally. It is also involved in supporting the implementation of the systems; and provides help desk support services to users of intellect software systems. In addition, the company provides software development services in the areas of wholesale banking comprising corporate core, commercial loan, payments, liquidity management, investments, virtual accounts, trade and supply chain finance, receivables and collections, treasury, corporate escrow deposits, and corporate banking exchange. Further, it offers consumer banking, including core banking, lending, cards, collection management, and digital engagement; wealth management; financial advisory, such as brokerage, custody, and transfer agency; insurance, such as magic submission, risk analyst, and underwriting; and bank treasury.
