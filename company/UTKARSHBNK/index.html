About Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited

Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited provides banking and financial services in India. It operates through Corporate/Wholesale Banking, Retail Banking, Treasury, and Other Banking Operations segments. The company offers savings, salary, current, term and recurring deposit, escrow, and RERA banking accounts; term, fixed, and recurring deposits; locker facilities; digital products; investment products; joint liability group, business, MSME, commercial vehicle and construction equipment, gold, personal, housing, individual, group, and other loans, as well as loans for the purchase of commercial properties; wholesale lending; overdraft against fixed deposit, LAP retail, and Micro LAP; outward remittances; positive pay; UPI payment solutions; and bill and credit card bill payment services. It also provides institutional, NRI, micro, retail, corporate, and wholesale banking; cash management; branch, internet, mobile, and WhatsApp banking services; digital onboarding; online account opening facilities; ATMs and micro-ATMs; debit and credit cards; mutual funds; and life, health, and general insurance.
