About Bajaj Electricals Limited

Bajaj Electricals Limited provides consumer products and lighting solutions in India. It offers ceiling, table, pedestal, wall, personal, tower, lifestyle, and domestic exhaust fans; ceiling lights, smart products, inverter lights, lamps, battens, decorative lights, accessories, outdoor lights, and portable lights; and indoor and outdoor lighting products. The company also provides water heaters/geysers, such as storage, instant, gas water heaters, as well as immersion rods; air coolers, including desert, tower, personal, and window coolers; dry and steam irons and garment steamers; room heaters; and kitchen appliances comprising mixers, juicers, table blenders, stand mixers, oven toaster grillers, microwave ovens, coffee makers, air fryers, electric cookers and kettles, toasters, hand blenders, and choppers and hand mixers. In addition, it offers cooking essentials consisting of induction and infrared cooktops, gas stoves, pressure cookers, and cookware; and spare parts and accessories, as well as male and female grooming products. The company offers its products under the Bajaj, Nex, Morphy Richards, and Nirlep brands. The company was formerly known as Radio Lamp Works Limited…
