About Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited

Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited, a financing institution, engages in the provision of financial assistance services in India. The company offers financial assistance by way of loans and investment in debt instruments to tourism/ hospitality, manufacturing, residential/commercial real estate, NBFCs/HFCs/MFIs, and other sectors, as well as social infrastructure, such as educational institutions, hospitals, etc, It also provides finance to non-banking and housing finance companies for secured onward lending. Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited was incorporated in 1989 and is based in New Delhi, India.
