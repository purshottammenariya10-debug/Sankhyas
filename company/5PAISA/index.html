About 5paisa Capital Limited

5paisa Capital Limited provides an online technology platform for trading in National Stock Exchange of India Limited, BSE Ltd, and MCX in India. The company offers financial services, including depository services; distributes mutual funds, bonds, and debentures; and equity and mutual fund research, and investment advisory services. The company offers 5paisa trading app, an all-in-one app for trading and investing; trader's terminal, an online trading platform; Xstream API, an API platform; margin trading facility; Algospace, an an algorithmic trading platform; 5paisa EXE, a desktop-based platform; Publisher Library, a website with minimal coding; 5paisa MCP, an AI trading companion; T+5; FnO 360, a trading terminal for derivative traders; Scalper terminal, a scalping interface with instant keyboard; and Trade on charts, a trading view chart. The company offers its services through a web-based trading terminal, and mobile application.It serves retail investors, high-net-worth individuals, non-resident Indians, and self-employed professionals. The company was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Thane, India.
