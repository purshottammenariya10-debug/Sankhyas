About Jindal Drilling & Industries Limited

Jindal Drilling & Industries Limited engages in providing drilling and related services to the oil and gas exploration companies in India. It operates through three segments: o¬ffshore drilling jack-up rigs built for efficiency, reliability and safety; directional / horizontal drilling / MWD services; and mud logging services. The company was incorporated in 1983 and is headquartered in New Delhi, India.
