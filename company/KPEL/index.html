About K.P. Energy Limited

K.P. Energy Limited provides balance of plant solutions for wind energy sector in India. The company offers solutions for wind project and wind-solar hybrid power project development, which comprises site identification and acquisition, site preparation and logistics, erection, power evacuation, and operations and maintenance services, as well as engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning services. It has a portfolio of 48.5 MW operational renewable energy assets, including 37 MW of wind and 11.5 MW DC solar energy assets. K.P. Energy Limited was incorporated in 2010 and is based in Surat, India.
