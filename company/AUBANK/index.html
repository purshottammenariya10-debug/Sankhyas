About AU Small Finance Bank Limited

AU Small Finance Bank Limited provides various banking and financial services in India. The company operates through Treasury, Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, and Other Banking Operations segments. It offers savings, NRI, corporate salary, senior citizen, women and family, minor, current, premium banking, special offerings, RERA and escrow, collection, share capital and dividend, EXIM, institution current, government banking, Forex, SNRR, and grouping of business and personal accounts; and fixed, recurring, FCNR, and green fixed deposits. The company also provides home, car, commercial vehicle and equipment, gold, personal, two-wheeler, tractor, micro business, working capital, term, commercial vehicle, and tractor loans; loan secured by vehicle, and against FD and property; supply chain finance, renewables funding, real estate funding and project finance, and NBFC and startup funding; and trade finance, such as letter of credit, bank guarantees, buyer's credit, export credit, and Forex and hedging solutions.
