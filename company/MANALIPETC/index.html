About Manali Petrochemicals Limited

Manali Petrochemicals Limited manufactures and sells petrochemical products in India, the European Union and United Kingdom, and internationally. The company provides propylene glycol, polyether polyol, Polyester Polyols, and related products; and flexible slabstock, flexible cold cure, rigid, and elastomers used for various applications in automobile, refrigeration and temperature control, adhesive, sealant, marine coatings, industrial rollers, furniture, and textile industries, as well as propylene oxide, an input material for the derivative products. It also, provides propylene glycol for use in pharmaceuticals, food, flavor, and fragrance industries used in medicines, food processing, cosmetics, personal care, flavours, and fragrances, while other grades support applications in paints, coatings, and polymer resins; and for production of polymer resins, carbonless paper, and automobile consumables, such as brake fluids and anti-freeze liquids. In addition, it offers propylene glycol mono methyl ether, a solvent used in paints and coatings, and electronics industries.
