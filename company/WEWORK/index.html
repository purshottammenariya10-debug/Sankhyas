About WeWork India Management Limited

WeWork India Management Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the rental and operation of office buildings and coworking spaces in India. The company offers office spaces, including private and managed offices; coworking spaces; and value-added services such as fit-out customization, events and hospitality, F&B services, technology services, overages, shoots and studios, parking, and solutions for global capability centers. It also provides advertising services through digital signage, ads in community newsletters, tabletops and standees, in-app and email notifications, and brand promotional spaces. In addition, the company operates Rivet, a design and build workspace company that provides existing site analysis and technical due diligence, spatial and space type planning, design concepts, electro-mechanical design, building management system integration and IoT design, arts and graphics, wayfinding and signage, project management, testing and commissioning, and documentation and handover of fit-out services.
