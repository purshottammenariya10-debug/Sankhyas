About Valplast Technologies Limited

Valplast Technologies Limited provides engineering and management services in India. It offers specialty services for water tightness of underground structure against pressurized and non-pressurized water, and injection and grouting works in tunneling, infrastructure, and hydraulic structures; and injection grouting, including ground improvement, new construction, and repair and retrofitting/renovation of old structures. The company is also involved in tunnel rehabilitation; tunnel construction, such as earthwork excavation, rock bolting, shotcreting, and design of blastless track; and coastal protection, slope protection, precast, site induction and safety, and on-site coordination. In addition, it provides pre-cast concrete systems and MEP engineering services for various types of infrastructure projects comprising underground structures, tunnels, landfills, dams, channels, shafts, canals, reservoirs, buildings, and other civil engineering projects. The company was incorporated in 2014 and is based in Noida, India.
