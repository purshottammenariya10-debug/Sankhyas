About Silver Touch Technologies Limited

Silver Touch Technologies Limited provides information technology solutions in India, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, and France. The company offers enterprise software services, including digital engineering, enterprise architecture, cloud applications development, mobile applications, and enterprise DevOps; and managed security services, such as SOC-as-a-service, cloud security, email security and protection, VAPT service, endpoint security, website and app security, security awareness training, identity and access management, and managed detection and response services. It also provides applied AI, data analytics, blockchain and IoT, and robotic process automation; modern workplace services comprising data center, communication, and IT consulting services, as well as backup and disaster recovery and call center/IP telephony; and SAP business one, SAP S/4 HANA, SAP successfactors, hosted cloud solution for SAP, and Odoo ERP.
