About Sudarshan Chemical Industries Limited

Sudarshan Chemical Industries Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells organic, inorganic and effect pigments, performance colorants, pearlescent pigments, and specialty chemicals in India and internationally. It operates through two segments, Pigments and Others. The company offers architectural, automotive, floor, industrial, wood, coil, glass, and powder coating products, as well as area marking and traffic paints; pigment solutions for personal care, stationary, cosmetics, automotive, consumer goods, packaging, construction and electrical, and fiber; and liquid, paste, and specialty printing inks. It also provides color printers, toners, and inkjets; and corrosion protection products. In addition, the company colorants, process chemicals, and specialized additives designed for anodizing, coloring, and sealing aluminum; and latex and paper coloration, seed coloration, crop protection, textile printing, concrete and plaster, leather coloration, and viscose coloration products. It also manufactures pollution control equipment, size reduction equipment, and grinding equipment for industrial applications; and clean air, grinding, and power handling solutions.…
