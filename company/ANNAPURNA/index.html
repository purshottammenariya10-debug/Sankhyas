About Annapurna Swadisht Limited

Annapurna Swadisht Limited manufactures and sells food products in India. It offers potato chips, fryums, namkeen, extruded snacks, noodles, biscuits, candy, and sweets. The company was incorporated in 2015 and is based in Kolkata, India.
