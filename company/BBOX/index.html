About Black Box Limited

Black Box Limited provides information and communications technology solutions in India, the United States, and internationally. It operates through System Integration, Technology Product Solution, and Others segments. The company offers enterprise networking, data centers, digital workplace, connected buildings, and cyber security products; keyboard, video and mouse (KVM), cables, Internet of Things (IoT), and audio video products; and IP-led hardware and software offerings, including KVM switches, OEM-distributed equipment, and contract-manufactured solutions. It also provides connectivity infrastructure, including structured cabling and fiber connectivity, passive infrastructure and network pathways, MDF/IDF modernization, IoT/OT connectivity and physical security infrastructure, and campus and multi-site network infrastructure; and enterprise networking with local and wide area, cloud, and wireless and private LTE networks; customer and employee experience, and advisory and consulting services. Guidance & outlook

Growth & demand

  • The quarter also saw our order bookings of US$339 million, taking our order backlog to a record ever of approximately US$950 million, up 83% year-on-year and providing strong visibility for future growth.
  • Around INR12,000 crores of this growth is expected to come organically, supported by strong execution, conversion of our robust order backlog, sustained demand for AI-led digital infrastructure, deeper engagements with strategic customers and improved project execution.

Margins & costs
