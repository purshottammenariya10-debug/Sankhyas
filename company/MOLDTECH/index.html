About Mold-Tek Technologies Limited

Mold-Tek Technologies Limited provides civil and mechanical design engineering services in India and internationally. It offers construction services, such as structural steel detailing services; connection design and delegated design services; precast detailing services; miscellaneous steel detailing services; structural steel estimating services; and pre-engineered steel building design. The company also provides manufacturing services, including automotive design services, special purpose machine design, and sheet metal press tool designs; and utilities comprising telecom tower structure design, power transmission design, and poles and towers. In addition, it offers plant engineering comprising process engineering, piping engineering, mechanical engineering, civil and structural engineering, electrical engineering services, and instrumentation engineering. Mold-Tek Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is headquartered in Hyderabad, India.
