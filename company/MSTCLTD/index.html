About MSTC Limited

MSTC Limited engages in providing e-commerce and scrap recovery services in India. The company operates in two segments, Marketing and E-Commerce. It is involved in e-procurement, e-auction, tender, recycling, and e-sale business, as well as trading business. The company also engages in disposal of ferrous and non-ferrous scrap, surplus stores, minerals, agri and forest produces, etc. The company was incorporated in 1964 and is headquartered in Kolkata, India.
