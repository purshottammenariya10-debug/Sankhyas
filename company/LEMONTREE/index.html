About Lemon Tree Hotels Limited

Lemon Tree Hotels Limited, together with its subsidiaries, owns, leases, operates, and franchises hotels in India. It offers accommodation and room, dining, bar, banquet, conference, meeting rooms, spa, fitness center, swimming pool, etc. hotel services. The company also provides housing rental and digital transformation services. It operates hotels under Aurika Hotels and Resorts, Lemon Tree Premier, Lemon Tree Hotels, Red Fox Hotels, Keys Prima, Keys Select, and Keys Lite brands. Lemon Tree Hotels Limited was incorporated in 1992 and is based in New Delhi, India.
