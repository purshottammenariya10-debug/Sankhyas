About Centum Electronics Limited

Centum Electronics Limited designs, manufactures, and exports electronic products In Europe, Middle East, Asia, and North America. It engages in design, development, production, and manufacturing of energy management controllers, electrical AC and DC converters, embedded computers, test means, digital and hardware in the loop simulators, actuator controllers, and smart actuators for auxiliary functions. The company also provides satellite bus systems, test tools, power management, and LRU and data recorders; onboard computer systems, receiver and seeker electronics, transmit receive modules, high power RF amplifier, digital receivers, ELINT controllers, power conditioning and processing unit, and radar finger printing systems; and energy conversion and storage systems, passenger information systems, and specialist engineering services and manufacturing services.
