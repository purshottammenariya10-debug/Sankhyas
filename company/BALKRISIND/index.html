About Balkrishna Industries Limited

Balkrishna Industries Limited manufactures and sells tires under the BKT brand in India, Europe, North America, and internationally. The company provides manufacturing and selling of off-highway tires (OHT) for use in various applications, such as agriculture, construction, earthmoving, mining and quarrying, industry, and port operations. It also offers two-wheeler, passenger car radial, and commercial vehicle radial tyres, as well as carbon black. Balkrishna Industries Limited was incorporated in 1961 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
