About HY-TECH ENGINEERS LIMITED

Hy-Tech Engineers Limited designs, manufactures, and supplies hydraulic fittings to various industrial applications in India and internationally. The company provides DIN-metric, JIC flared and flareless, O-Ring Face Seal, and conversion fittings, as well as fittings customized to customer specifications. It serves various applications needs in construction machinery, automotive, agricultural machinery injection molding machines, and hydraulic systems, as well as railways and defense industries. The company was incorporated in 1978 and is based in Thane, India.
