About RSWM Limited

RSWM Limited operates as a textile manufacturer in India, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, South East and Far East Asia, and the United States. The company operates through Yarn and Fabric segments. It offers cotton, synthetic, core-spun, hollow-core, zero-twist, melange, eco-friendly, specialty, and recycled yarns used for knitting, weaving, suiting, shirting, home furnishings, carpets, sewing threads, and industrial textiles. The company was formerly known as Rajasthan Spinning & Weaving Mills Ltd. RSWM Limited was incorporated in 1960 and is headquartered in Noida, India.
