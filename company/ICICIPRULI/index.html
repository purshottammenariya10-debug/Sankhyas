About ICICI Life Insurance Limited

ICICI Life Insurance Limited provides life insurance, annuity, pension, and health insurance products to individuals and groups in India. The company offers term, savings, unit linked, guaranteed return, child education, and retirement insurance plans; and pension fund management services. It distributes its products through individual and corporate agents, banks, and brokers, as well as through proprietary sales force (PSF) and online channels. The company was formerly known as ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited and change its name to ICICI Life Insurance Limited in September 2026. The company was incorporated in 2000 and is based in Mumbai, India. ICICI Life Insurance Limited is a subsidiary of ICICI Bank Limited.
