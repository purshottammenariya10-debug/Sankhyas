About CCL Products (India) Limited

CCL Products (India) Limited engages in the production, trading, and distribution of coffee and related products in India. The company offers filter, premix, instant, and flavored coffee, including spray dried coffee powder and agglomerated coffee, freeze dried coffee, freeze concentrated liquid coffee, roast and ground coffee, and roasted coffee beans under the Continental brand. It also exports its products. The company was incorporated in 1961 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
