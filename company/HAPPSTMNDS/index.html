About Happiest Minds Technologies Limited

Happiest Minds Technologies Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides information technology (IT) solutions and services in India, the Americas, Australia, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and Africa. It operates in three segments: Infrastructure Management and Security Services (IMSS); Product and Digital Engineering Services (PDES); and Generative AI Business Services (GBS). The IMSS segment provides end-to-end infrastructure and security solutions comprising advisory, transformation, managed and hosted services, and secure intelligence solutions with specialization in cloud, virtualization and mobility across a multitude of industry verticals and geographies. Its PDES segment provides platform development, device engineering, quality assurance, experience engineering, and technology acceleration for enterprises and digital natives. The GBS Services segment offers process automation services, workflow optimization services, and predictive analysis services to integrate Generative AI into business processes. Happiest Minds Technologies Limited was incorporated in 2011 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
