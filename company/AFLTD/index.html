About Autofurnish Limited

Autofurnish Limited engages in the manufacture, trade, export, and supply of automobile accessories under the Autofurnish and Mototrance brand names in India. It provides four-wheeler accessories, including body covers, foot mats, trunk mats, organizers, tissue boxes, seat cushions, seat gap fillers, perfumes and fresheners, and other accessories, as well as LTM foot mats and steering covers. The company also offers two-wheeler accessories, such as body covers, face masks, riding gears, seat covers, scooter mats, and helmets. In addition, it trades in car utility products, audio accessories, lights, spare parts, interior accessories, and dashboard products. The company also sells its products through its online store. Autofurnish Limited was founded in 2012 and is based in New Delhi, India.
