About The India Cements Limited

The India Cements Limited manufactures and sells cement and cement-related products in India. The company offers grey and white cement, ready mix concrete, and clinkers. It also engages in the generation of power from windmills and thermal power plants; and provision of freight services, including ship hiring and chartering services. The company was incorporated in 1946 and is based in Chennai, India. The India Cements Limited operates as a subsidiary of UltraTech Cement Limited.
