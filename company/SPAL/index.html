About S.P. Apparels Limited

S.P. Apparels Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and exports knitted garments for infant wear, children's apparel, and intimate wear in India and internationally. The company offers yarns, fabrics, and cotton waste products under the Angel & Rocket brand. The company also provides dyeing, embroidery, and printing services. In addition, it engages in the manufacture, distribution, marketing, and retail sale of menswear garments under the Crocodile brand name. S.P. Apparels Limited was founded in 1988 and is headquartered in Tirupur, India.

Concall summary (2026-08-17)

AI summary of the earnings call transcript · tone: Positive · source document

Guidance & outlook

  • Based on current customer discussions, our receipt order visibility, expected capacity ramp -up and planned utilization levels, we remain confident of achieving our previously stated consolidating revenue guidance of INR 2,000 crores for FY27.
  • Consolidated EBITDA for Q1 FY27 stood at INR 61.4 crores compared to INR 52.9 crores in Q1 FY26, reflecting a growth of 15.9% year-on-year.

Growth & demand

  • Our U.K. business delivered revenue of INR 33.3 crores in Q1 FY27, registering strong growth of 125.2% year -on-year.
  • Finally, coming to the Retail division, SP Retail Ventures reported revenue of INR 18.83 crores in Q1 FY27, reflecting a growth of 26.7% year-on-year.

Margins & costs

  • EBITDA margin improved 15.3% compared to 13.1% in Q1 FY26, supported by better operating efficiency and improved margin performance.
  • Segmental performance, our Garment division, including the Young Brand Apparel reported an adjusted operational revenue of INR 337.3 crores for the Q1FY27 with an adjusted EBITDA of INR 59.2 crores and the EBITDA margin stood at 17.6%.

Capex & expansion

  • The quarter was moderate from a revenue perspective, but profitability remained strong and we saw better operating efficiencies, improved product mix and healthy yarn spreads, which supported margin expansion.
  • Alongside our capacity expansion initiatives, we continue to optimize our sourcing and manufacturing model.

Balance sheet & cash

  • I'm pleased to inform our shareholders that the Board has approved a dividend of INR 3 per share for the year and has also proposed a stock split of the company's equity shares from a face value of INR 10 per share to INR 2 per share, subject to the necessary approval.
  • On a standalone basis, gross debt stood at INR 258 crores.
