About Physicswallah Limited

Physicswallah Limited offers online and offline coaching and study material for test preparation of various competitive exams in India. It offers various courses for competitive exams, including IIT JEE, NEET, ESE, GATE, AE/JE, Olympiad, UPSC, and state PSC; government exams, such as judiciary, SSC, defence, teaching, JAIIB and CAIIB, railway, nursing, banking; undergraduate and postgraduate entrance exams that includes MBA, IPMAT, IIT JAM, law, CUET UG, UGC NET, GMAT, design and architecture, pharma, CUET PG, NEET PG, CSIR NET, NIMCET, and CLAT PG; CA, CS, ACCA, CFA, and finance courses; BSc and B.com, mobile and PC, IELTS, and TOEFL; and pre-primary, elementary, secondary, and higher secondary schools. The company also provides various study materials, books, notes, CBSC online preparation, and NCERT solutions; and other courses for agricultural, chess mate, online degrees, and art learning, as well as programes to support students studying hight education in abroad, includes AcadFly.
