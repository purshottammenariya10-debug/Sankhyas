About Unicommerce eSolutions Limited

Unicommerce eSolutions Limited provides a suite of software-as-a-service products in India and internationally. It offers Inventory Management System, a platform for viewing and processing details of inventory across different location, and Warehouse Management System, a software that manages the stock location and level of the goods in a warehouse. The company also provides Multichannel Order Management System, which enables management of bulk orders and purchases across multiple channels, including offline stores, online marketplaces, and carts; Omni-Channel Retail Management System, an omni channel order and inventory management solution; B2B order management software; and Quick Commerce, which manages moving parts of quick commerce, such as order capture and fulfillment. In addition, it offers UniReco, a returns and payment reconciliation platform; UniCapture, a video management system; and e-commerce dashboard and reports platforms, as well as the Uniware mobile app for multichannel retail. Unicommerce eSolutions Limited was formerly known as Unicommerce eSolutions Private Limited. The company was incorporated in 2012 and is based in Gurugram, India.
