About Tips Music Limited

Tips Music Limited engages in the acquisition and exploitation of music rights in India and internationally. The company involved in the production and acquisition of audiovisual music content and monetisation of the content library digitally. It also provides live events and concerts services. The company was formerly known as Tips Industries Limited and changed its name to Tips Music Limited in September 2024. The company was founded in 1975 and is based in Mumbai, India.
