About Elin Electronics Limited

Elin Electronics Limited engages in the design, manufacture, and sale of electronics motors, tools, moulds, dies, kitchen appliances, personal care and lighting products, and automotive components in India and internationally. The company offers LED lighting, fans, and switches, including LED Lighting and flashlights, fans, switches; small appliances, such as dry and steam irons, mixer grinders, hand blenders, bar blenders, electric kettles, toasters, hair dryers, hair straighteners, trimmers, and electric sterilizers and heated hair brushes; medium appliances, including air fryers, oven toaster grills, oil-filled radiators, kitchen chimneys, and air coolers; and fractional horsepower motors. In addition, the company provides medical diagnostic cartridges and diagnostic devices; and molded and sheet metal parts and components, such as plastic moulded sheet, metal parts, and components for auto ancillary and consumer durable sectors. It serves original equipment manufacturers and original design manufacturers. The company was founded in 1969 and is based in New Delhi, India.
