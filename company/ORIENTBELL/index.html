About Orient Bell Limited

Orient Bell Limited manufactures, trades in, and sells ceramic, wall, and floor tiles in India and internationally. The company also offers bathroom, kitchen, room, marble, vitrified, flexi, and subway tiles. It sells its products through channel partners. The company was incorporated in 1977 and is headquartered in New Delhi, India.
