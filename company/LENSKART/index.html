About Lenskart Solutions Limited

Lenskart Solutions Limited, a technology-driven eyewear company, engages in designing, manufacturing, branding, and retailing of eyewear products in India, Japan, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and internationally. Its products include prescription eyeglasses, sunglasses, goggles, screen glasses, and other products, such as contact lenses and eyewear accessories, including frames for men, women, and kids. The company also engages in lens manufacturing, lens edging, lens design, frame design, frame manufacturing, and delivery. It operates as a direct-to-consumer company that designs and sells a wide range of eyewear products under the Lenskart and Owndays brands; and sub brands, including John Jacobs, Vincent Chase, Hooper, Lenskart Air, Hustlr, LPL, and Meller. It sells its products through online and offline channels, such as retail stores, websites, mobile applications, and other channels. The company also owns and operates eyewear retail stores in India and provides home eye check-up services. Lenskart Solutions Limited was incorporated in 2008 and is based in Gurugram, India.
