About Landmark Cars Limited

Landmark Cars Limited engages in the automotive retail business in India. It provides dealership services for Mercedes-Benz, BYD, MG Motor, Mahindra & Mahindra, Kia, Honda, Renault, Volkswagen, Jeep, Citroën and Ashok Leyland. The company also sells new and pre-owned passenger vehicles; provides after-sales service and repairs, including sale of spare parts, lubricants, and accessories; and facilitates the sales of third-party financial and insurance products. Landmark Cars Limited was founded in 1998 and is based in Mumbai, India.
