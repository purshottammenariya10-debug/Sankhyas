About Kellton Tech Solutions Limited

Kellton Tech Solutions Limited engages in the provision of digital transformation, ERP, and other IT services in APAC, Europe, the United States, and internationally. The company offers AI services, including AI development, generative AI, agentic AI & automation traditional AI, AI engineering & platforms, and AI governance and risk data engineering for AI services; data and analytics services comprising data consulting, data migration and modernization, analytics services, and integration and API services; product strategy, consulting, design, and management; and cloud engineering services, such as consulting, migration, and manage services, as well as DevSecOps services. It also provides product engineering services, including digital application development, mobile engineering, IoT and wearables solutions, and quality engineering services; blockchain, web 3.0, and metaverse services; ServiceNow services comprising AI enterprise, implementation, optimization, and consultation services; and SAP services, such as AMS support, automation, security and GRC, value-added solutions, and other SAP implementation services, as well as S/4HANA implementation services.
