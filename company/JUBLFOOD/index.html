About Jubilant FoodWorks Limited

Jubilant FoodWorks Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in food service business in India, Turkey, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Azerbaijan, and Georgia. The company operates quick service restaurants under the Domino's, Dunkin', Popeyes, Hong's Kitchen, and COFFY brands. It also engages in wholesale and retail sale of food items. The company was formerly known as Domino's Pizza India Ltd. and changed its name to Jubilant FoodWorks Limited in September 2009. Jubilant FoodWorks Limited was incorporated in 1995 and is based in Noida, India.
