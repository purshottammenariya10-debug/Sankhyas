About Sasken Technologies Limited

Sasken Technologies Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides product engineering and digital transformation services in India, North America, Europe, the Middles East, and the Asia Pacific. It operates in two segments, Software Services and Product Solutions. The company offers hardware design and development, platform and application software, and modem and embedded software development; application development, device and platform testing, and android services; ADAS and autonomous, body electronics and autosar, integrated cockpit, and telematics, V2X, and smart mobility; and off-highway machines, connected manufacturing, machine tools, and enterprise devices. It also provides mobility solutions; terminal design and development, as well as access network enhancement, maintenance, and IP; radio access networks; and fare collection and revenue management, mobility as a service, real-time passenger information, planning and dispatch, asset management, and passenger safety services.
