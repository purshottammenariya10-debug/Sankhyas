About City Union Bank Limited

City Union Bank Limited provides various banking products and services in India. It operates through four segments: Treasury, Corporate/Wholesale Banking, Retail Banking, and Other Banking Operations. The company offers savings, current, staff salary, and resident foreign currency accounts; fixed, recurring, flexifix, desire, and income tax saver deposits; personal, gold, agricultural, consumer, home/housing, property, education, working capital, business, and foreign currency loans, as well as loans against property, gold-based overdraft, cash credits, bill finance, bank guarantee, and letter of credit; international business services, including export, import, FX retail, remittance, correspondent banking, and treasury risk management services; sovereign gold bonds; debit, prepaid, and credit cards; NRI banking products and services; and insurance products comprising life, health, and general insurance products, as well as social security schemes.
