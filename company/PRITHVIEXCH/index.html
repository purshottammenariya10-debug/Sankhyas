About Prithvi Exchange (India) Limited

Prithvi Exchange (India) Limited engages in the foreign exchange business under the Prithvi Exchange brand in India. The company deals in tradeable foreign currencies, traveller cheques, drafts, and swift transfers. It also operates as an authorized agent to receive Money Transfer send through Western Union MoneyTransfer, Money Gram, and XpressMoney. In addition, the company offers forex cards for business travel; card loading; business travel cards, and regulatory compliance and documentation; forex cards for leisure travel; doorstep delivery of foreign exchange; and remittances services. Further, it provides various services, such university fee and MICE payments; guaranteed investment certificate (GIC) account, an account that demonstrates financial stability to Canadian immigration; overseas film shooting forex support; and CTM/crew wages remittances. The company serves leisure and business travelers, students studying abroad, it, manufacturing, services, corporates, frequent travelers, families with loved ones overseas, as well as banks, travel agents, and channel partner.
