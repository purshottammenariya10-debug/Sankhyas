About Mallcom (India) Limited

Mallcom (India) Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells personal protective equipment in India and internationally. The company offers head protection equipment comprising safety helmet, shell bump cap, base ball cap, ear plugs, safety goggles, and face mask, such as respirator, surgical, hygiene, and face mask with valve; and gloves, including black and yellow, cut an sewn nitrile, cut, heat, and impact resistant, Canadian, cryogenic, driver, knitted, latex, leather, mechanical, nitrile, PU, seamless and sustainable nitrile, thermal, and welder gloves, as well as leather accessories. It also provides disposable, denim, flame retardant, heavy, hi vis, light, multinorm, occupational, soft shell and polo, sustainable, thermal, and women's workwear; and safety shoes.
