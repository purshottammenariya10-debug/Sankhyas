About Stove Kraft Limited

Stove Kraft Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of kitchen and home solutions in India and internationally. The company offers pressure cookers, cookware, cooktop, induction and infrared cooktops, chimney, and hobs; mixer grinders, wet grinders, nutri mixer, airfryer, airfryer + oven, coffee maker, cold press juicer, kettles, oven, pop up toaster, sandwich maker, rice cooker, and portable blender; and chopper, idiyappam maker, water bottles, lunch boxes, and kitchen tools. It also provides vaccuum cleaner, car solutions, flood mops, garment care, room heater, emergency lamps, and extension chord; hair dryer, trimmer, and massagers; lockers; and pedestal, mini, tower, and wall fan. In addition, the company offers franchise services. It sells its products under the Pigeon, Gilma, and BLACK+DECKER through retail outlet, general trade, modern trade, e-commerce, and quick commerce. It also exports its products. Stove Kraft Limited was founded in 1994 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
