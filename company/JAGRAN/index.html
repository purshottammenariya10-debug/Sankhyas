About Jagran Prakashan Limited

Jagran Prakashan Limited prints and publishes newspapers and magazines in India. It operates through three segments: Printing, Publishing and Digital; FM Radio; and Others. The company offers Dainik Jagran, a daily newspaper; Inext; Nai Dunia & Nav Dunia Rank Hindi newspapers; Mid-day, a compact newspaper; Inquilab; Mid-day Gujarati, a Gujarati newspaper; Punjabi Jagran; Sakhi editorial panel; and Khet Khalihaan, an agricultural magazine.
