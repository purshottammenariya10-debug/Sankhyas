About Hinduja Global Solutions Limited

Hinduja Global Solutions Limited engages in technology, AI, and business services in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Europe, India, and internationally. The company operates through Business Process Management and Digital, Media, and Communications segments. It provides voice and non-voice based services, such as contact center solutions and back-office transaction processing, as well as operates NXTDIGITAL that provides satellite, digital cable, and broadband services.
