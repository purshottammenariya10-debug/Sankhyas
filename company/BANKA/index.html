About Banka BioLoo Limited

Banka BioLoo Limited engages in the wastewater treatment business in India. The company is involved in setting up, operation, and maintenance of faecal sludge treatment plants, wastewater treatment plants, and effluent treatment plants. It also offers biotoilet, biodigester, and footloo; and packaged sewage treatment plant and sewage treatment plant. In addition, the company provides Urban SaaS, a STP as a service for wastewater management in urban areas; and Pluto Geobag Fecal Sludge Treatment Plant. Further, it engages in design, installation, monitoring, and maintenance of bio-toilet for Indian railways. The company serves government and public, real estate and commercial developers, commercial and retail, and industrial and corporate customers. The company was incorporated in 2012 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
