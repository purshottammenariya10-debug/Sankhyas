About Capital Small Finance Bank Limited

Capital Small Finance Bank Limited provides a range of banking and financial products and services for individuals and businesses in India. It operates through four segments: Treasury, Wholesale Banking, Retail Banking, and Other Banking Operations. The company offers various deposits accounts, including savings, current, cumulative, short term, quarterly and monthly interest deposit and recurring deposits; real estate regulatory authority accounts; term deposits; bank lockers; debit and credit cards; trade finance; bank guarantees; 3-in-1 trading account; ATMs; cheque and pass books; and pension scheme. It also provides loans, such as loan against rentals, auto, housing, gold, personal, mortgage loan against property, credit card for farmer, corporate loan, and MSME loans. In addition, the company offers various insurance products, such as general, life, health, travel, home, and motor. Further, it provides money transfer, bill payment system, payment gateway, net banking, tax payments, and foreign exchange, government banking, NRI banking services and point of sale services.
